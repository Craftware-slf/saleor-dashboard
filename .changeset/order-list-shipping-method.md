Order list has a "Shipping method" column: the order's shipping method name, the Dropp pickup point read from the `dropp_pickup_point_name` / `dropp_pickup_point_address` metadata the Örninn storefronts stamp, and the warehouse for click & collect. On by default for new list settings; staff with a saved column layout can add it from the column picker.
